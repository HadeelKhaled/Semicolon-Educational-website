Computer History

Some important dates, the stages of the development of the computer during three different periods and the emergence of several generations of the computer

  • 1-
    Hand
    Tools

  • 2000 BC

    It is the early stage, which extends until the middle of the seventeenth century, and is characterized by the development of manual arithmetic devices and the scientific production reached by many scientists in the field of mathematical and physical sciences. It is divided into three time periods.

  • 825

    The book “Algebra and Muqabala” was written by the scholar Abu Jaafar Muhammad bin Musa Al-Khwarizmi, who is the author of the idea of written methods and steps to carry out arithmetic operations to obtain the required results.

  • Twelfth Century

    The use of Indian and Arabic counting systems spread, which was distinguished by its emphasis on the value of each number according to its position within the number. The Arab counting system is characterized by the presence of a zero, unlike the Roman counting system, which does not contain a zero.

  • 2-Mechanical and electromechanical computers

  • 1642

    The invention of a semi-automatic mechanical machine by the French scientist Blaise Pascal that he used to collect numbers and it consisted of metal wheels engraved on the circumference of each of them and at regular intervals with decimal numbers.

  • 1806-1890

    The English scientist Charles Babbage designed a mechanical calculator (the difference machine) capable of performing arithmetic operations and some other operations (logarithms, and the trigonometric ratios of angles using the difference of squares of numbers).

  • 1944

    Aiken and Grace Hopper, with the help of IBM, were able to produce the first electro mechanical calculator called Mark-1.

  • 3- Electronic computers

  • 1945

    A team of engineers at the University of Pennsylvania led by John Mosely and Eckert produced a computer at Moore College of Electrical Engineering and called it (ENIAC: Electronic Numerical Integrator and Calculation).

  • 1946

    Production of a device called (EDVAC: Electronic Discrete Variable Automatic Computer) the scientist John von Neumann, who focused his efforts on logic and programming and came up with the idea of storing the program and data inside the computer's memory in the form of numbers in the binary system.

  • 1951

    1951: Production of a device called (Universal Automatic Computer) Developed by Mosley and Eckert after joining UNIVAC, it is the first device to be produced and marketed on a commercial basis.

  • Computer Generations

  • First generation (1940-1959)

    Characteristics Of The First-Generation Computers

    1-The production of machines depended on the use of single elements, the most important of which was the electronic vacuum valve.
    2-The size of the machines here was large and of great weight, characterized by slowness.

  • Second Generation (1959-1964)

    Characteristics Of The Second Generation Computers

    1- The transistor has replaced the vacuum tube as it is characterized by its small size, long life, and it does not require high power to operate in addition to its high reliability.
    2- The speed of execution of operations is close to hundreds of thousands of operations / seconds
    3- Use High-level programming languages

  • Computer Generations

  • Third Generation (1964-1970)

    Characteristics Of The Third Generation Computers

    1-Its size is much smaller than the previous one and the cost is little.
    2-The main memory capacity in some devices has reached 8 million bytes.
    3-The operating systems have been updated. The idea of virtual memory has been used, as well as the multi-programming system and the multi-processor system

  • Fourth Generation (1972-2010)

    Characteristics Of The Fourth Generation Computers

    Computer became very small in size, it became portable.It is much faster and accuracy became more reliable. The production cost reduced to very low in comparison to the previous generation. It became available for the common people as well.
